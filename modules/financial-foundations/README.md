# Financial Foundations

Optional, jurisdiction-neutral decision support for indie builder collaborations. It offers common considerations for thinking through a revenue split, tracking expenses, and estimating when the project might pay for itself. It is not accounting, tax, or financial advice, and it does not produce figures you should rely on without your own analysis.

This module is optional. The core kit works without it.

---

## Section 01. What's included

- **revenue-split-calculator.md** -- Worksheet for choosing a split model, modeling scenarios at different revenue levels, and recording the decision.
- **expense-tracker.md** -- Monthly expense log with shared vs. personal classification, running totals, and reimbursement tracking.
- **break-even-worksheet.md** -- A framework for thinking through fixed costs, variable costs, revenue per sale, and whether the timeline works for both collaborators.

## Section 02. Scope and exclusions

This module covers basic financial visibility and decision support for a two-person collaboration. It provides conversation frameworks and things to consider, not financial models or calculations you should rely on without your own analysis.

It does not cover multi-currency modeling, complex tax scenarios, tiered equity models, financial projections beyond basic break-even thinking, jurisdiction-specific tax or reporting advice, or accounting determinations presented as universally applicable.

Cross-border financial complexity and meaningful revenue are professional-advice triggers (see "When This Kit Stops Being Enough" in the README).

## Section 03. How to use it

Work through the revenue split calculator together when you first set up the collaboration. Start the expense tracker on day one. Use the break-even worksheet once you've decided what (if anything) you're selling.

All three feed into the [Working Agreement](../../templates/working-agreement.md) and the [Contribution Log](../../08-contribution-log.md).
