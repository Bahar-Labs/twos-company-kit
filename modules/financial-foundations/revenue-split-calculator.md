# Revenue Split Calculator

A worksheet for two collaborators to think through how revenue gets distributed. These are decision tools, not accounting or legal recommendations. Your project may have different costs or assumptions. Decide together what belongs in your model.

## Section 03. Decide what gets deducted before splitting

Things collaborators commonly consider:

- Refunds and chargebacks
- Payment and transaction processing fees
- Required taxes or withholding where applicable
- Approved shared project expenses (see [05 Expenses](../../05-expenses.md))
- A project reserve, if the collaborators agree to maintain one
