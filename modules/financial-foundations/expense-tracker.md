## Section 04. Guidelines

1. Log at the time of purchase. Don't batch three months of expenses from memory.
2. Include receipts for anything over the agreed approval threshold.
3. Classify "shared" vs. "personal" per the collaborators' agreement, see [05 Expenses](../../05-expenses.md).
4. Review together monthly alongside the [Contribution Log](../../08-contribution-log.md).
