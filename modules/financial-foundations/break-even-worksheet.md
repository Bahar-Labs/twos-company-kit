# Break-Even Worksheet

When does this collaboration start paying for itself? This worksheet helps you think through that question together. Your project will have its own costs and assumptions. Use this as a conversation framework, not a financial projection.

---

- Hosting or deployment platform fees
- Domain registration (annualized to monthly)
- Tool or software subscriptions used for the project

## Section 04. Think through the timeline

Using your estimates above, consider:

- Roughly how many sales per month would cover the ongoing costs?
- How many total sales would it take to recoup everything spent before first revenue?
- How many months might that take?

These are rough estimates to help you have a realistic conversation, not precise projections.

---

## Section 05. Reality check

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| How many sales per month do you realistically expect in month 1? | |
| Roughly how many months until you expect to break even? | |
| Can both collaborators sustain the project for that long without revenue? | |
| Is the break-even timeline acceptable to both collaborators? | |

If the answer to the last two questions is "no," revisit the cost structure (can you stay on lower-cost tiers longer?), the pricing (is it too low?), or the scope (can you ship something smaller first?).

---

## Section 06. Store and revisit

Store the completed worksheet with the project's other financial records. Revisit it quarterly or whenever costs or pricing change significantly. Compare your estimates to actuals.
