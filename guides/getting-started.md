# Getting Started with Two's Company Kit

Governance exists to support what you're building, and Two's Company Kit exists to support both.

You do not need to complete every template before you start building. The goal is to make the important decisions visible before they become problems.

---

> **Keep these concepts separate**
>
> - **Access** → *Can I do it technically?*
> - **Responsibility** → *Am I accountable for it?*
> - **Approval authority** → *Can I authorize the decision?*
> - **Platform role** *(e.g., GitHub Owner)* → *What administrative permissions does the platform give me?*
> - **IP treatment** → *Who is intended to have what rights in the asset?*

## Section 01. Before you start: get your own copy

Copy the templates you need into your own private repository or other access-controlled location before filling anything in. Don't fork the Two's Company Kit repository for that purpose. See "How to use it" in the [README](../README.md#section-05-how-to-use-it).

---

## Section 02. Minimum viable Two's Company Kit

If you do nothing else before starting:

- Complete the [Contribution Inventory](../templates/contribution-inventory.md)
- Decide intended IP treatment ([02 Who Owns What](../02-who-owns-what.md))
- Agree on money ([03 Money](../03-money.md))
- Identify critical systems and their custody and recovery paths ([04 Infrastructure and Custody](../04-infrastructure-and-custody.md))
- Agree on what happens if someone exits ([06 What Happens When](../06-what-happens-when.md))
- Capture those decisions in the [Working Agreement](../templates/working-agreement.md)

Everything below adds depth, structure, resilience, and records. But those six things are the floor.

---

## Section 03. The full walkthrough

### Step 1. Start with the Handshake

Each collaborator completes the [Contribution Inventory](../templates/contribution-inventory.md) separately. See [01 The Handshake](../01-the-handshake.md).

### Step 2. Compare what you're bringing

Talk through overlaps, gaps, expectations, time, and boundaries. The comparison conversation in [01 The Handshake](../01-the-handshake.md) tells you what to look for.

### Step 3. Inventory the project assets

Use the [Project Asset Inventory](../templates/project-asset-inventory.md) while working through [02 Who Owns What](../02-who-owns-what.md). Record what exists, who created it, who holds it, and the intended treatment.

### Step 4. Decide money and expenses

Work through [03 Money](../03-money.md) and [05 Expenses](../05-expenses.md). Use the [Financial Foundations](../modules/financial-foundations/README.md) tools if helpful.

### Step 5. Map infrastructure and access

Complete the [Access and Custody Matrix](../templates/access-and-custody-matrix.md) and identify critical systems. See [04 Infrastructure and Custody](../04-infrastructure-and-custody.md).

### Step 6. Talk about exits before anyone wants one

Work through [06 What Happens When](../06-what-happens-when.md). The post-exit rights checklist is the important part.

### Step 7. Create the Working Agreement

Use the [Working Agreement template](../templates/working-agreement.md) to summarize the governing rules and point to the living supporting records. See [07 Working Agreement](../07-working-agreement.md) for what to include.

### Step 8. Set up the project records

Keep the supporting records in the agreed governance-record location:

- [Contribution Inventory](../templates/contribution-inventory.md)
- [Project Asset Inventory](../templates/project-asset-inventory.md)
- [Access and Custody Matrix](../templates/access-and-custody-matrix.md)
- [Expense Tracker](../modules/financial-foundations/expense-tracker.md) (optional, part of Financial Foundations)
- [Contribution Log](../templates/contribution-log.md)
- [Project Directory](../templates/project-directory.md)

These are living documents maintained separately from the Working Agreement. See the "Store completed records appropriately" guidance in the [README](../README.md).

### Step 9. Set up GitHub governance (if using GitHub)

Follow the [GitHub Setup Guide](github-setup.md). Start at Level 1.

### Step 10. Start the Contribution Log

Then actually build the thing.
