# 08 -- Contribution Log

The boring thing nobody does until they wish they had.

Start this on day one. Update it weekly at minimum. It takes five minutes and saves hours of disagreement later.

---

## Section 01. Why this exists

Memory is unreliable. Two months from now, neither of you will accurately remember who built what, who paid for what, or how many hours each collaborator put in. The log is the shared record. It's governance evidence: a record of contributions, not a universal legal determination of ownership or entitlement. It's also the thing that makes handoffs possible if someone steps away (see [06 What Happens When](06-what-happens-when.md)).

Revenue models that depend on proportional contribution ([03 Money](03-money.md), Model 2) need this log. Exit reviews ([06 What Happens When](06-what-happens-when.md)) are cleaner when there's a record. Monthly expense reviews ([05 Expenses](05-expenses.md)) happen alongside this.

---

## Section 02. How to use it

Use the [contribution log template](templates/contribution-log.md). One table per month. Each row is a meaningful contribution. Don't log every micro-task. Do log anything you'd want credit for later.

**Categories:** `build` (code, design, content creation), `ops` (deployment, infrastructure, maintenance), `biz` (marketing, outreach, business development, sales), `money` (payments made or received, distributions), `admin` (documentation, governance, planning)

---

## Section 03. Ground rules

1. **Log your own work.** Don't log what the other collaborator did.
2. **Be honest about hours.** If you spent 2 hours, don't write 4. If you spent 6, don't write 3 because it "shouldn't have taken that long."
3. **Log expenses immediately.** Include the amount, the currency, and what it was for. If you use the optional Financial Foundations module, this feeds the expense tracker.
4. **Review together monthly.** Five-minute check-in: does the log look right? Anything missing? Any concerns about the balance of contributions?
5. **This is data, not a scoreboard.** The log informs governance decisions. It's not ammunition.
