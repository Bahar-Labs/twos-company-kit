# 07 -- Working Agreement

This is the record of the decisions you made while working through [01](01-the-handshake.md) through [06](06-what-happens-when.md). Both collaborators review it, agree it's accurate, and store it in the project's chosen governance-record location.

This template is not legal advice or a substitute for qualified professional advice. Depending on how you complete and use it, your decisions may create legal obligations. If the collaboration reaches any of the formalization triggers described in the README, take this document and the rest of your project records to appropriate professionals.

Use the [working agreement template](templates/working-agreement.md) as a starting point. The sections below explain what to capture.

---

## Section 01. What to include

### Project identity

What is this project? Who is it for? One paragraph.

### Collaborators

Name, entity (if any), location and timezone, contact information.

### What each collaborator brings

Summarize the inventories from [01 The Handshake](01-the-handshake.md). What pre-existing work, skills, time, and distribution each collaborator contributes.

### Intended IP treatment

Summarize decisions from [02 Who Owns What](02-who-owns-what.md). Pre-existing work, jointly created work, modules, and the project's license.

### Infrastructure custody

Summarize the [access and custody matrix](templates/access-and-custody-matrix.md) and the principles in [04 Infrastructure and Custody](04-infrastructure-and-custody.md). Who custodies the critical systems, who has recovery access or what succession path applies, and what happens to access on exit.

### Money

Summarize decisions from [03 Money](03-money.md). Revenue split model, percentages, deductions applied before splitting, distribution cadence and method, and when the split gets revisited.

### Expenses

Summarize decisions from [05 Expenses](05-expenses.md). What counts as a shared expense, approval thresholds, pre-revenue cost handling, and the expense cap.

### Exit and transition decisions

Summarize decisions from [06 What Happens When](06-what-happens-when.md). Inactivity thresholds, notice periods, post-exit rights and obligations for each major category.

### Decision rights

Who can make routine project decisions without the other collaborator's explicit approval? What requires both collaborators' approval? Who may enter vendor or customer commitments on behalf of the project?

Entries marked *(recommended)* are starting points most two-person collaborations adopt, not requirements. Change any of them if your collaboration needs something different. Blank rows have no common recommendation and are yours to decide.

These recommendations are about process, meaning who is involved in a decision before it takes effect. They do not determine what any collaborator is entitled to, what anyone's rights are, or what obligations exist between you. Those remain yours to decide and record.

| Changes to revenue split or financial terms | Both collaborators *(recommended)* |
| Adding a new collaborator | Both collaborators *(recommended)* |

| Major strategic pivots | Both collaborators *(recommended)* |

### Deadlock handling

If the collaborators cannot agree on a material decision, what happens? Options include: a trusted third party mediates, one collaborator has final say within their domain, or the disagreement triggers the fork or exit process from [06 What Happens When](06-what-happens-when.md).

### How the Working Agreement changes

Material changes to the Working Agreement should be proposed through a pull request (or equivalent visible, reviewable change) and approved by all collaborators before taking effect.

### Formalization triggers

Record which triggers from the README's "When This Kit Stops Being Enough" section are most likely to become relevant. Agree on a governance review cadence (example policy: every six months, or when a trigger becomes material).
